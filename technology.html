Cryopreservation

OPTIMA™: solving the cold-chain challenge for 3D tissues.

Complex in vitro models only reach widespread and clinical use if they can be reliably cryopreserved. We mapped the unique challenges of freezing organ-on-chip and organoid systems, structures that demand barrier-eliminating interface designs.

That work became OPTIMA™: engineered for large tissue models, standardised cryo-storage and global cold-chain distribution.

Self-organisation

Letting organoids do what they do best.

Multi-Organoid Patterning and Fusion (MOrPF) guides organoids into patterns and lets them fuse naturally, forming continuous lumens without scaffolds. OPTIMA™ applies the same principle: imperceptible tissue interfaces that support self-assembly instead of overriding it.
